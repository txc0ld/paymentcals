Conveyance duty (stamp duty)

Find out how much conveyance duty (stamp duty) you may pay when you purchase a property.

About conveyance duty (stamp duty) Payable when you buy property in the ACT, whether it’s a home, land, or a commercial property.
Conveyance duty for non-commercial property Find out how conveyance duty (stamp duty) applies to residential and rural property transfers.
Conveyance duty for commercial property Find out how conveyance duty (stamp duty) applies to commercial property transfers.
Pay your conveyance duty Find out the different ways you can pay your conveyance duty (stamp duty).
Conveyance duty exemptions Find out if you may be eligible to pay less (or no) conveyance duty (stamp duty) when buying a property.
Commercial lease duty Find out how conveyance duty (stamp duty) may apply when you pay a premium for a commercial lease.
Aggregating transactions for conveyance duty Find out how conveyance duty (stamp duty) is calculated when transferring more than one property.
Partitions of land and conveyance duty Find out how conveyance duty (stamp duty) is calculated when a property is split between owners.
